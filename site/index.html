Narayan Narayan! Your tabs travel across every loka.

Send tabs between your browsers. No server in the middle.

Narada moves the tabs you pick, and if you choose, your sign-in, from one browser to another: Chrome to Firefox, Edge to Brave, laptop to desktop. It's peer-to-peer and end-to-end encrypted, it needs no account, and it collects nothing.

How it works, in four panels

Why Narada

🕊️

No middleman

There are no Narada servers and no accounts. After a brief handshake over public relays, your browsers talk directly.

🔐

Locked down

It's end-to-end encrypted, with pinned device keys, forward secrecy and replay protection, layered on WebRTC's own encryption.

✋

You approve everything

Every incoming transfer waits for your OK, and sign-in data only moves when the sender explicitly includes it.

🍪

Stay signed in

Optionally carry cookies and site storage with a tab, so it opens logged in on the other browser.

🌐

Any combination

Chrome, Edge, Brave, Opera, Vivaldi, Firefox, LibreWolf and more, in any pairing.

🙈

Collects nothing

There's no analytics, telemetry or tracking. Read the privacy policy.
